# SA2 V3.1 Preregistration: Low-FPR Operating Point

**Registered:** 2026-11-01, after the V3 blind evaluation and before any V3.1 fitting.

## Motivation (from V3 blind, post-hoc)

At the **frozen** thresholds from 1% out-of-fold training FPR, PE-v3 had higher TPR and higher FPR than the baseline. At **matched** false-positive rates on the same blind scores (threshold re-picked on blind rows, descriptive only), PE-v3 had **lower** TPR than the baseline at 0.5% and 1.0% FPR. V3.1 therefore targets score calibration and class balance, not only a higher detection rate at a looser threshold.

## Fixed elements (unchanged from V3)

- Model configuration: `lgbm__num_leaves-15_min_child_samples-20_feature_fraction-0.7` (selected in V3 search).
- Feature sets: candidate `pe_v3` (2,105 columns, no timestamp); baseline `pe_v1s` (274 columns, stable import buckets).
- Training core: frozen V1 `train` split plus all `new_train` rows from the V3 cohort (1,228 rows). The V3 `blind` split is never used for training or threshold fitting.
- Decision rule (confirmatory): same as V3 — TPR gain of at least 2 percentage points, paired group-bootstrap 95% CI lower bound above 0, and candidate FPR no higher than baseline FPR at frozen thresholds.

## V3.1 interventions (all registered here)

1. **Isotonic calibration** on pooled group-CV out-of-fold scores on the full training pool (both arms). The operational threshold is applied to calibrated scores.
2. **Training FPR target 0.5%** on calibrated out-of-fold scores (both arms).
3. **Sample weights** on LightGBM fits: weight 3.0 for 64-bit malicious rows (`pe1s_machine` = 34404), 1.0 otherwise.
4. **Additional training data:** MalwareBazaar PE samples not in V1 or the V3 cohort, with at least 50% PE64 (`die_filetype` = PE64) in the newly added malicious rows. Benign files exclude all paths and SHA-256 values already used in V1 or V3.
5. **New blind cohort:** a second group-aware split (`v3.1` manifests under `artifacts/v3.1/`). No SHA-256 or `group_id` overlap with the combined training pool. V3 blind files are excluded from this cohort.

## Non-goals

- No change to the LightGBM hyperparameter grid.
- No use of V3 blind false-positive paths to select training files.
- No Binary Ninja extraction for new files.

## Reporting

- Primary confirmatory metrics: fixed-threshold TPR/FPR on the V3.1 blind cohort.
- Secondary (descriptive): matched-FPR TPR on blind scores, as in the V3 post-hoc analysis.
