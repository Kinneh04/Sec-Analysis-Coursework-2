# SA2 V3.1 Results: Calibration and Low-FPR Operating Point

**Date:** 2026-11-01  
**Outcome:** The confirmatory V3.1 blind run did **not** support an improvement claim. Isotonic calibration with a 0.5% training FPR target produced thresholds near 0.99 and **zero detections** on the blind cohort for both arms. A development-only replay without isotonic showed better low-FPR ranking but still did not beat the baseline at a fixed threshold.

## 1. Confirmatory blind (frozen V3.1)

| Arm | TPR (95% CI) | FPR (95% CI) | ROC-AUC | PR-AUC |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| Baseline `pe_v1s` | 0.000 (0.0–0.007) | 0.00% (0–2.4%) | 0.973 | 0.990 |
| Candidate `pe_v3` | 0.000 (0.0–0.007) | 0.00% (0–2.4%) | 0.973 | 0.989 |

- **Blind size:** 733 files (159 benign, 574 malicious), 676 groups. Disjoint from the 3,337-row training pool (V1 train + V3/V3.1 `new_train`).
- **Decision rule:** not met (TPR gain 0, CI not above 0).
- **Limitation:** only **159 benign** blind rows met the “unseen group” rule after excluding every directory group and imphash already present in V1 or the V3 cohort. At this size, the minimum attainable FPR is about 0.63% (one false positive).

### Matched-FPR (descriptive, blind only)

| Target FPR | Baseline TPR | Candidate TPR | Delta |
|------|------|------|------|
| 0.5% | 0.437 | 0.000 | −0.437 |
| 1.0% | 0.730 | 0.775 | **+0.045** |

At 1% FPR the candidate ranked slightly better, but the frozen operating point was unusable.

## 2. What V3.1 changed (per `docs/v3.1_preregistration.md`)

1. Isotonic calibration on pooled group-CV out-of-fold scores.  
2. Training FPR target **0.5%** on calibrated scores.  
3. Sample weight **3.0** for 64-bit malicious rows.  
4. **+1,592** training rows from V3.1 `new_train` (50% PE64 in newly sampled malware).  
5. New blind cohort under `artifacts/v3.1/` (V3 blind untouched).

Training pool after expansion: 3,337 rows (1,838 benign, 1,499 malicious), 741 groups.

Out-of-fold training at the frozen thresholds was already weak (candidate TPR about 0.25 at 0.11% FPR), which indicated that isotonic mapping plus a 0.5% FPR target drove scores toward extreme thresholds.

## 3. Development replay (not confirmatory)

Replay on the **same** V3.1 blind scores with **isotonic disabled** and a **1%** training FPR target (`artifacts/v3.1/development/dev_replay.json`):

| Metric | Baseline | Candidate |
|------|------|------|
| Blind fixed TPR | 0.690 | 0.554 |
| Blind fixed FPR | 0.00% (0/159) | 0.63% (1/159) |
| Matched TPR @ 1% FPR | 0.740 | 0.780 (+4.0 pp) |

This suggests:

- **Ranking at 1% FPR** may favor PE-v3 after more training data.  
- **Fixed-threshold transfer** still fails without careful calibration.  
- **Isotonic on high-dimensional GBDT scores** on this pool harmed threshold generalization.

## 4. Interpretation

| Finding | Implication |
|------|------|
| V3 blind: higher TPR, higher FPR at frozen thresholds | Operating-point shift, not a free improvement |
| V3 matched-FPR @ 1%: candidate **worse** than baseline | PE-v3 did not dominate at equal FPR on the first blind cohort |
| V3.1 confirmatory: 0 TPR at frozen thresholds | Isotonic + 0.5% target was too aggressive |
| V3.1 matched-FPR @ 1%: candidate **+4.5 pp** | Some low-FPR ranking gain with more data; not preregistered |
| Small benign blind (159) | FPR estimates are noisy; need unseen **directory groups**, not just unseen files |

## 6. Directions for a future V3.2 (not executed)

Would require a **new preregistration** and a **new blind cohort** with enough unseen benign **groups** (for example benign samples from sources not represented in V1/V3, or a finer group key that does not collapse entire vendor trees).

- Drop isotonic; use **1%** out-of-fold FPR with the enlarged benign calibration set (1,838 benign rows).  
- Keep 64-bit malicious up-weighting and PE64-enriched malware sampling.  
- Consider **Platt scaling** on a held-out group fold rather than pooled isotonic.  
- Primary confirmatory metric unchanged: fixed threshold from training, same decision rule as V3.

No SOTA or product superiority claim is made.
